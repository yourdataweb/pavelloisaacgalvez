The hall where half of Vilanova plays

A municipal indoor facility for basketball, handball, futsal and volleyball. Courts for training and match days, seating and step-free access. Home to the town's teams.

Facilities

What is inside the hall

A covered main court with seating, built for both league competition and school or training use.

Main court
Covered, with seating, for basketball, handball, futsal and volleyball.
Changing rooms
Changing rooms and showers for teams and officials, opening off the court.
Spectator seating
Seats for the public, with space reserved for wheelchair users.
Step-free access
Accessible entrance and parking for people with reduced mobility.
Training area
Space for warm-ups and training off the main court.
Sports grounds
Part of a sports zone shared with other municipal facilities.

Sports

What you can play

The hall hosts the local leagues and the town clubs' training sessions through the season.

  • Basketball

    A regulation court for club and school matches and training.

  • Handball

    One of the facility's regular disciplines.

  • Futsal

    An indoor court for local leagues and training games.

  • Volleyball

    Net and enough space for competition and practice.

  • School sport

    PE sessions and activities during the school day.

  • Competition

    Venue for official fixtures of the town's teams.
